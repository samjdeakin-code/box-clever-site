Support Privacy

Privacy policy

For Box Clever on iPhone and iPad. Last updated 12 September 2026.

The short version: Box Clever collects no personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your scores and settings stay on your device. The one thing that can leave your device is your Daily Quiz score, sent to Apple's Game Center, and only if you have chosen to sign in to Game Center.

What the app stores

Box Clever keeps your quiz results, streaks, settings and a record of which questions you've seen. This is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is not sent anywhere, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center

The Daily Quiz has a leaderboard provided by Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, Box Clever submits your daily score to Apple so it can be shown on that day's leaderboard alongside your Game Center player name. This is handled entirely by Apple's GameKit framework under Apple's privacy policy. Box Clever does not receive, store or process any Game Center data itself. If you are not signed in to Game Center, nothing is submitted and the app works exactly the same without a league.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for an account, email address or any personal details.
  • It does not show adverts or contain advertising software.
  • It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites, and does not use the advertising identifier.
  • It does not include any third-party software development kits.
  • It makes no network requests of its own. All questions are built into the app and it works fully offline.

Sharing your score

After the Daily Quiz you can share your result using the standard iOS share sheet. What you share, and with whom, is entirely your choice; the app only prepares the text.

Children

Box Clever is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date, and the app's listing on the App Store will reflect it.
